Case Study Spotlight

The Obsidian Pavilion: 30-Foot Cliffside Cantilever in Mumbai

Perched over coastal basalt rock in Bandra West, this build required twenty-two 60-foot micropiles drilled into volcanic rock to anchor a post-tensioned foundation slab.

The Challenge

Severe coastal seismic zone, high monsoon humidity, and zero street-level crane access.

The Solution

Modular steel spider crane assembly and continuous concrete pumping over 300 linear feet.
