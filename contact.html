Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on our process, costs, and project milestones in India.

Common questions answered for property developers and homeowners.

How does Stackly handle cost overruns in India?

We operate primarily on guaranteed maximum price (GMP) contracts backed by comprehensive 3D BIM modeling. By resolving spatial clashes digitally before site execution, change orders are virtually eliminated.

Can Stackly build from existing architect plans?

Yes. Approximately 60% of our projects are designed by outside architectural studios. We collaborate seamlessly as the general contractor to execute their vision with uncompromising fidelity.

How do clients track daily site progress?

Every client receives secure login credentials to the Stackly Client Portal, featuring live HD jobsite camera streams, daily progress logs, 3D drone scans, and budget burn charts in INR.

What warranties are provided upon handover?

Stackly provides an industry-leading 10-year structural warranty on all foundation and framing assemblies, alongside a 2-year comprehensive waterproofing and finish warranty.
