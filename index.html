Construction Lifecycle

From initial site survey to keys in your hand.

A structured 5-stage project delivery roadmap engineered to eliminate surprises and cost overruns.

01

Site & Geotech

Soil testing, topographic scans, environmental reviews, and site feasibility analysis.

02

Engineering & Permits

3D BIM structural drawings, municipal authority filings, and RERA/fire permit expediting.

03

Groundwork & Core

Excavation, grading, deep pilings, and foundation structural concrete pours.

04

Superstructure

Framing, mechanical, electrical, plumbing rough-in, and weather-tight building envelope.

05

Commissioning

Finishes, acoustic testing, HVAC commissioning, and formal handover package.
