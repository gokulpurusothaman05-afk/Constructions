Division 03

BIM 3D Modeling, Structural Engineering & Municipal Approvals

Before breaking ground, we construct your entire building digitally in millimeter-accurate 3D BIM space to eliminate field clashes.

01

Clash Detection

Automated spatial analysis prevents MEP ductwork, plumbing, and structural beam collisions prior to site execution.

02

Seismic & Wind Simulation

Advanced finite element calculations ensure your structure withstands Indian Seismic Zone III/IV/V conditions and monsoon gale loads.

03

Statutory Approvals

Deep municipal coordination with BMC, BBMP, DTCP, fire departments, and environmental boards for smooth clearances.

Division 05

Heritage Restoration & Structural Retrofitting

Preserving architectural history requires forensic sensitivity combined with modern structural engineering. Stackly reinforces colonial stone facades in South Mumbai, restores heritage wooden rafters in Bengaluru, and inserts modern VRV HVAC systems without damaging historical fabric.

Specializations: Lime-mortar tuckpointing, basalt stone remediation, carbon-fiber structural wraps, and heritage committee compliance.
