Our Story

From boutique timber framing in Mumbai to India's premier design-build firm.

Stackly was born out of frustration with conventional Indian construction fragmentation — where architects, liaising consultants, and general contractors operated in friction. We proved that by combining deep structural engineering with architectural sensitivity, builds are completed on time, within budget, and with zero aesthetic compromise.

01

Integrated Thinking

Every project estimator is a qualified structural engineer. Cost modeling in INR happens in real-time alongside spatial design, ensuring no concept is unbuildable.

02

In-House Craftsmanship

Unlike broker contractors, we maintain direct in-house crews for critical trades: mass concrete pouring, heavy timber joinery, and architectural structural steel.

03

Zero-Compromise Standards

We do not cut corners behind walls. Our MEP infrastructure, waterproofing membranes, and building envelopes are built to endure for 100+ years.
